About Us

Who We Are

Booker’s Club is a library for a community that loves reading and learning the old-fashioned way. A library with a catalog of books and an environment designed to help you relax and enjoy expanding your knowledge and imagination.

We built a library management system to make browsing, tracking, and managing the book collection simple for everyone be it the users and administrators.

Our Goal

To revive the spirit of reading and books. To connect readers with books much more efficiently To keep an organized and welcoming library. To provide ease to users and admins alike so they can find what they need and want.

About Us

Who We Are

Booker’s Club is a library management system built to make browsing, tracking, and managing the book collection simple for everyone be it the users and administrators.

Our Goal

We want to connect readers with books much more efficiently and keep the library organized, so users can find what they need and admins can manage the inventory system with ease.

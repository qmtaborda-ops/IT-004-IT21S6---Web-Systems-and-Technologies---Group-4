Terms and Conditions

By submitting a borrow request, you agree to the following conditions:

  1. A borrow request must include a borrow date and a return date.
  2. The return date must not be earlier than the borrow date and must be within two months of it.
  3. A request is subject to librarian confirmation. The book is not considered borrowed until the request is approved.
  4. Books must be returned by the agreed return date. Late returns incur a fine that is determined by the price of the book.
  5. The borrower is responsible for the book while it is in their possession and must report damage or loss immediately.
  6. Damaged or missing books may result in a liability equal to the listed price of the book.
